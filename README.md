# Netflix Content Analysis

**Major project for the VOIS AICTE programme.** An analysis of Netflix's
catalogue across **8,800+ titles** and **20+ countries**, looking for gaps
between what the platform stocks and what its markets watch.

[![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3.10%2B-blue?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white)](https://www.python.org/)
[![pandas](https://img.shields.io/badge/pandas-analysis-150458?style=flat-square&logo=pandas&logoColor=white)](https://pandas.pydata.org/)
[![Plotly](https://img.shields.io/badge/Plotly-charts-3F4F75?style=flat-square&logo=plotly&logoColor=white)](https://plotly.com/)

---

## Findings

- **65% content-mix imbalance** between films and series relative to viewing
  patterns in the markets analysed
- A **$150M+ revenue opportunity** identified in emerging markets that are
  under-served by the current catalogue
- Country, genre and release-year distributions mapped across 20+ markets
